Weekend Mornings

Weekend Breakfast Buffet in Ottawa

Join KS on the Keys for our weekend breakfast buffet in Ottawa South every Saturday and Sunday morning — featuring made-to-order omelettes, waffles, pancakes, eggs benedict & coffee.

Weekend Breakfast Buffet

Every Saturday from 10:00am–2:00pm
Sunday from 9:00am–2:30pm

Buffet Pricing

  • Adults: $22.95
  • Seniors (65 and older): $19.95
  • Kids under 12 years old: $14.95

Traditional Breakfast Items

Omelettes made to order, scrambled eggs, eggs benedict, hard boiled eggs, bacon, sausage, toast, home fries, hash browns, breakfast pastries, yogurt, muffins, croissants.

Waffle / Pancake / Crepe Station

Waffles, pancakes, crepes, French toast, English cream, banana slices, mixed berries, chocolate sauce, syrup, jams, and honey.

Beverages

Coffee, tea, juice, and pop are included with your buffet.

Note: Prices and food selection are subject to change without notice. Please note, if you are still eating your Breakfast buffet while the Brunch buffet begins, you are welcome to enjoy the Brunch items, however, the Brunch prices will apply.

Do you offer halal options?
Yes! All chicken and beef dishes on our menu are 100% halal, and we also offer halal pepperoni on our pizzas. Please ask your server if you have any questions about specific dishes.
Are there vegetarian and vegan options?
Absolutely! We have a wide variety of vegetarian and vegan dishes across our menu — from Vegan Thai Cauliflower Bites and Vegan General Tao Cauliflower to Vegan Spring Rolls, Vegan Hummus, and vegan pizza options with vegan cheese. Look for the VEGAN badge on our menu items.
Do I need a reservation?
Walk-ins are always welcome! However, for groups over 10 people, we strongly recommend making a reservation in advance. You can book online through our website or call us at 613-521-0498.
Can you accommodate large groups?
Yes! We have private spaces that can accommodate groups from 12 to 90 guests. Our Private Event Room holds up to 90 guests, our Semi-Private Mezzanine holds 25 to 60 guests, and our Private Board Room holds 12 to 24 guests. For large group bookings, email RSVP@ksrestaurant.ca.
Do you offer catering off-site?
Yes! We offer full catering services both at our venue and off-site throughout the Ottawa area. Our team handles everything from setup to cleanup. Contact us at RSVP@ksrestaurant.ca to discuss your needs and get a custom quote.
Are you open on Mondays?
We are generally closed on Mondays for regular dining. However, for large group events and private bookings, we can open on Mondays by special arrangement. Please reach out to us at RSVP@ksrestaurant.ca to arrange a Monday booking.
