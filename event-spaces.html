Private Event Venue in Ottawa

Looking for a private event venue in Ottawa? KS on the Keys in South Keys offers three beautifully appointed private and semi-private rooms for 12 to 90 guests, with 100% halal catering and free on-site parking.

Quick Facts — Private Event Venue in Ottawa

  • Capacity: 12 to 90 guests across 3 rooms (Private Event Room up to 90, Mezzanine 25–60, Board Room 12–24)
  • Price From: Group buffets from $45/person, plated dinners $54/person, or hourly room rental
  • Halal & Dietary: 100% halal chicken and beef, plus vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options
  • Parking & Location: Free on-site parking at 1029 Dazé Street, South Keys, Ottawa
  • How to Book: Inquire online, email RSVP@ksrestaurant.ca, or call 613-521-0498
