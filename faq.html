Everything You Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about dining, halal & dietary options, reservations, private event rooms, catering, and weekend buffets at KS on the Keys.

General

Where is KS on the Keys located?
KS on the Keys is located at 1029 Dazé Street, Ottawa, ON K1V 2G3. We are situated in Ottawa South, with free on-site parking available directly at our door.
What are KS on the Keys' opening hours?
We are open Tuesday and Wednesday from 11:00am to 8:00pm, Thursday and Friday from 11:00am to 10:00pm, Saturday from 10:00am to 10:00pm, and Sunday from 9:00am to 8:00pm. We are closed on Mondays. Please note that for large group events we can open earlier, later, and on Mondays — contact us to arrange.
How long has KS on the Keys been open?
KS on the Keys was founded in May 1971 by Kelly Swaita and has proudly served the Ottawa community since 1971. It is one of Ottawa's longest-running, family-owned restaurants, now operated by Robert Swaita.
How do I contact KS on the Keys?
You can reach us by phone at 613-521-0498, by email at RSVP@ksrestaurant.ca, or through our website at ksonthekeys.com. For event and catering enquiries, email is the fastest way to reach our team.
Is there parking at KS on the Keys?
Yes — KS on the Keys has free, ample parking directly on-site at 1029 Dazé Street. You will never need to worry about finding a spot or paying for parking when dining or attending an event with us. We also have overflow parking across the street at the South Keys Shopping Centre.
Is KS on the Keys family friendly?
Absolutely. KS on the Keys has been a family destination in Ottawa South since 1971. Our dining room welcomes guests of all ages, and our diverse menu ensures there is something for everyone at the table — from kids to grandparents.
Is KS on the Keys fully accessible?
Yes. KS on the Keys is fully accessible for guests with mobility needs. Our main dining room is ground-floor accessible, and our second-floor event space is accessible via elevator. We want every guest to feel comfortable and welcome.
Does KS on the Keys have a patio?
Yes! KS on the Keys has a patio available during the warmer months. It's a great spot to enjoy our daily specials and soak up the Ottawa summer. No reservations needed for patio seating — just walk in.
Does KS on the Keys have a loyalty rewards program?
Yes — KS on the Keys has a loyalty rewards app where you get 5% off your first order and earn points on every visit. Visit our Rewards App page or ask our team in-restaurant for details on how to download and start earning rewards.
What does KS stand for in KS on the Keys?
KS stands for Kelly Swaita, the founder of KS on the Keys. Kelly opened the restaurant in May 1971 with a vision of serving fresh, diverse food to the Ottawa community. Today, that vision continues under the Swaita family.
Has KS on the Keys always been in the same location?
KS on the Keys has been proudly rooted in Ottawa South since 1971. Our home is at 1029 Dazé Street, Ottawa, where we have built generations of memories for Ottawa families, businesses, and communities.
Is KS on the Keys locally owned?
Yes — KS on the Keys is a locally owned, family-run Ottawa restaurant founded in May 1971 by Kelly Swaita and operated by the Swaita family.
What area of Ottawa is KS on the Keys in?
KS on the Keys is located in Ottawa South at 1029 Dazé Street, Ottawa, ON K1V 2G3. We are centrally positioned to serve residents of Hunt Club, Alta Vista, Riverside South, Gloucester, Nepean, and the broader south Ottawa area. Free on-site parking makes us easy to reach from anywhere in the city.
Is KS on the Keys near Hunt Club, Ottawa?
Yes — KS on the Keys at 1029 Dazé Street is located very close to Hunt Club in Ottawa South, making us one of the most convenient and accessible restaurants for Hunt Club residents. Free on-site parking means you can drive over without any hassle.
Is KS on the Keys near Riverside South, Ottawa?
Yes — KS on the Keys is easily accessible from Riverside South and is a popular dining destination for Riverside South families and residents. We are one of the closest full-service restaurants with private event spaces to the Riverside South community.
Is KS on the Keys near Alta Vista, Ottawa?
Yes — KS on the Keys is conveniently located for Alta Vista residents in Ottawa South. Our central Ottawa South location makes us easily reachable from Alta Vista, Hunt Club, Riverside South, and surrounding neighbourhoods.
Are gift cards available for KS on the Keys?
Yes — KS on the Keys gift cards are available and make a perfect gift for any occasion. Treat a friend, family member, or colleague to a meal at one of Ottawa's most beloved restaurants. Ask our team in-restaurant for details on purchasing gift cards.

Menu & Dietary

What type of food does KS on the Keys serve?
KS on the Keys serves a diverse, crowd-pleasing menu rooted in Canadian comfort food, with something for everyone. We are known for our pizza, Chinese food, handhelds, and great appetizers — alongside salads, pasta, calamari, and a rotating selection of daily specials. We also offer Lebanese and Mediterranean options for those who enjoy those flavours. In addition to our regular a-la-carte menu, we offer special buffets for private events, on the weekends and on public holidays. There truly is something for everyone — which has been our philosophy since 1971.
Is KS on the Keys halal?
Yes — all of our beef and chicken is halal certified, and we also offer halal pepperoni on our pizzas, making us a welcoming dining destination for Muslim families and guests in Ottawa.
Does KS on the Keys have vegetarian and vegan options?
Yes. KS on the Keys has a wide range of vegetarian and vegan-friendly options on our menu. From fresh salads and appetizers to pasta, vegetable dishes, and vegan pizza options — plant-based diners are very well catered for. Please ask your server and they will be happy to guide you through the best options.
Does KS on the Keys have gluten-free options?
Yes. KS on the Keys has a wide range of gluten-free options on our menu, including a gluten-free pizza crust option. All of our gluten-free items have a symbol that says GF beside it in our menu. Please note that KS on the Keys is not a dedicated gluten-free facility, so cross-contamination is possible.
Can you accommodate food allergies or dietary restrictions?
We do our best to accommodate dietary restrictions and allergies. Please inform your server of any allergies or requirements when you arrive or when placing your order, and our kitchen team will do their best to assist you; if we cannot accommodate the dietary restriction we will notify you.
Does KS on the Keys have daily specials?
Yes — we run daily specials throughout the week, Tuesday through Sunday. Specials feature fresh, crowd-favourite dishes at great value. Check our Daily Specials page on our website or call us at 613-521-0498.
Can I order KS on the Keys for delivery?
Yes — KS on the Keys is available for delivery through Uber Eats, SkipTheDishes, and DoorDash. You can also order online for pickup directly through our website at ksonthekeys.com.
Can I order KS on the Keys for takeout?
Yes — KS on the Keys is available for takeout and pickup orders directly through our website at ksonthekeys.com or by calling in at 613-521-0498 to make your order.

Reservations

Do you take walk-ins or do I need a reservation?
Walk-ins are always welcome at KS on the Keys — no reservation needed. We recommend calling or booking ahead for groups of 6 or more to ensure we have the right space ready for you. You can reserve online at ksonthekeys.com or call us at 613-521-0498.
How do I make a reservation at KS on the Keys?
You can book a table for up to 10 guests online through our website at ksonthekeys.com. For groups of more than 10 guests, please use our 11+ Guests reservation form or contact us directly at 613-521-0498 or RSVP@ksrestaurant.ca and our team will arrange everything for you.
Do you accommodate large group dining?
Yes — KS on the Keys is well set up for large group dining. For groups of 11 or more, we recommend contacting us in advance so we can arrange the best seating and service for your group. We regularly host large family gatherings, birthday dinners, and group celebrations.

Events & Private Spaces

What private event spaces does KS on the Keys offer?
KS on the Keys has three private event spaces. Our Private Event Room accommodates up to 90 guests and is ideal for large family celebrations and parties. Our Semi-Private Mezzanine overlooks the restaurant and fits 25 to 60 guests — accessible by elevator. Our Private Board Room accommodates 12 to 24 guests and is equipped with a TV, speaker, and microphone, perfect for corporate meetings and intimate gatherings.
Is the event venue accessible for guests with mobility needs?
Yes — KS on the Keys is fully accessible. Our Private Event Room is on the ground floor, and our second-floor Semi-Private Mezzanine and Private Board Room are accessible via elevator. We want every guest at your event to feel comfortable and welcome.
Can I bring my own decorations to my event?
Yes — you are welcome to bring your own decorations to personalize your event space. Our team will work with you to ensure the room is set up to your liking. Please note that confetti, glitter, and open flame are not permitted. Please contact us in advance to discuss any setup requirements.
How far in advance should I book an event space?
We recommend booking your event space as early as possible, especially for weekends and popular dates like holidays and long weekends. For large events like weddings, we suggest booking at least 3–6 months in advance. For smaller corporate or private events, 4–6 weeks is generally sufficient.
How many guests can KS on the Keys accommodate for events?
Our private event rooms accommodate groups from 12 to 90 guests (Private Board Room: 12 to 24 guests; Semi-Private Mezzanine: 25 to 60 guests; Private Event Room: up to 90 guests), and larger buyouts can be arranged across the restaurant. Whether it is an intimate boardroom meeting or a large wedding reception, we have a space to suit your needs.
Do you host weddings at KS on the Keys?
Yes — KS on the Keys is a popular wedding venue in Ottawa South. Our Private Event Room accommodates up to 90 guests and our full catering team will work with you to create a custom menu tailored to your celebration. Larger weddings can be achieved by booking out additional spaces in the restaurant. Contact us at RSVP@ksrestaurant.ca to discuss your wedding.
Do you have corporate event facilities?
Yes. KS on the Keys offers three spaces perfectly suited for corporate events. Our Private Board Room accommodates 12 to 24 guests and is equipped with AV equipment including a TV, speaker, and microphone — ideal for meetings, presentations, and team lunches. Our Semi-Private Mezzanine accommodates 25 to 60 guests, and our Private Event Room accommodates up to 90 guests for larger corporate dinners, galas, and company celebrations.

Catering

What are your catering options?
KS on the Keys offers flexible in-house and off-site catering options to suit any event and any crowd. Choose from our custom buffets — perfect for larger gatherings where variety and abundance set the tone — or our set course menus for a more structured, elegant dining experience. Every menu is built around your guests' preferences and can be fully tailored to dietary requirements including halal, vegetarian, and vegan options. Feeding Ottawa since 1971, we know how to put together a spread that keeps everyone happy. Contact us at RSVP@ksrestaurant.ca to start building your custom menu.
Is there a minimum guest count for catering?
There is no minimum guest count for catering orders. For our buffet packages specifically, a minimum guest count applies. For all other catering formats — set course meals, custom menus, or off-site service — we are happy to accommodate events of any size. Contact us to discuss your requirements.
Do you provide serving staff for catering events?
Yes — KS on the Keys provides professional serving staff for catering events. Our team handles everything from setup to service to cleanup, ensuring a seamless and stress-free experience for you and your guests.
How far in advance do I need to book catering?
We recommend booking catering at least 2–4 weeks in advance to ensure availability and allow time to finalize your custom menu. Nonetheless we do accommodate last-minute catering requests. For large events or peak dates, earlier is always better. Contact us at RSVP@ksrestaurant.ca to get started.

Buffets

Does KS on the Keys have a weekend buffet?
Yes! We serve a Breakfast Buffet on Saturday and Sunday mornings as well as a Brunch Buffet on Sundays. Both buffets are a popular Ottawa South tradition and walk-ins are welcome, or you can reserve a table online.
What are the hours and prices for the Weekend Breakfast Buffet?
Our Weekend Breakfast Buffet is served Saturdays from 10:00am to 2:00pm and Sundays from 9:00am to 10:30am. Pricing is $22.95 for adults, $19.95 for seniors (65+), and $14.95 for kids under 12, and includes coffee, tea, juice, and pop.
What are the hours and prices for the Sunday Brunch Buffet?
Our regular Sunday Brunch Buffet is served every Sunday from 10:30am to 2:30pm ($32.95 for adults, $26.95 for seniors 65+, and $16.95 for kids under 12). On Sundays in December (December 6, 13, 20, and 27), we serve our Festive Sunday Brunch featuring carved turkey breast and roast beef au jus ($34.95 for adults, $28.95 for seniors 65+, and $18.95 for kids under 12).
