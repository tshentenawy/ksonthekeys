Sundays in December · Dec 6, 13, 20 & 27

Festive Sunday Brunch in December

Celebrate the holiday season every Sunday in December with our expanded festive buffet featuring carved turkey breast, roast beef au jus, and all your Sunday brunch favourites.

Book Your Table

Sundays in December: December 6, 13, 20 and 27

1.5 hour time limit per seating so everyone gets to enjoy the festive buffet.

Breakfast Buffet (9:00am to 10:30am)

  • Adults: $22.95
  • Seniors (65+): $19.95
  • Kids under 12: $14.95

Festive Brunch Buffet (10:30am to 2:30pm)

  • Adults: $34.95
  • Seniors (65+): $28.95
  • Kids under 12: $18.95

Festive Additions

Roast beef au jus, carved turkey breast, stuffing, garlic mashed potatoes, cranberry sauce, horseradish.

Traditional Breakfast Items

Omelettes made to order, scrambled eggs, eggs benedict, bacon, sausage, toast, home fries, hash browns, fruit, breakfast pastries, muffins, croissants.

Waffle / Pancake / Crepe Station

Waffles, pancakes, crepes, French toast, English cream, mixed berries, chocolate sauce, syrup, and jams.

Hot Brunch Station

Meat loaf, Chicken Florentine, assorted pizza, chicken Marsala, rice pilaf, General Tso chicken, vegetable fried rice, eggrolls, spring rolls, lasagna, swedish meatballs, penne cream sauce, chicken kabobs, potatoes, stuffed grape leaf rolls, hummus, chicken wings, steamed vegetables.

Cold Buffet & Lebanese Station

A variety of cold salads, Lebanese zaatar pies and cheese pies.

Dessert Station

Dessert squares, cake, cookies, Jello, pudding, fruit & ice cream.

Beverages

Coffee, tea, juice, and pop are included with your buffet.

Note: All chicken and beef dishes are 100% Halal. Prices and food selection are subject to change without notice. Please note, if you are still eating your Breakfast buffet while the Brunch buffet begins, you are welcome to enjoy the Brunch items, however, the Brunch prices will apply.
