The Details

  • Available November 1 to December 30, 2026.
  • New Year's Eve is a separate event with its own menu. Call us for details.
  • Buffet packages require a minimum of 25 adults.
  • The Board Room plated menu seats 12 to 24 guests.
  • A deposit confirms your date: $300 private room, $100 mezzanine, $100 board room.
  • Cancellations within 14 days of the event forfeit the deposit.
  • Final guest count is due seven days before your event and is the number you are billed for.
  • Gratuity of 18% applies to groups of eight or more, on all food and beverage.
  • Taxes are additional to all listed prices.
  • Dietary and allergen requests are accommodated with seven days' notice.
  • Buffet food may not be taken home.
  • Decor is welcome. Confetti, glitter and open flame are not.
