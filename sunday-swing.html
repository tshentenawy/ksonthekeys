Sunday Swing Christmas Show featuring the 18-piece Big Band Ottawa

Sunday, December 20, 2026  ·  5:00 pm to 7:30 pm  ·  family-friendly

About Sunday Swing @ KS on the Keys

Sunday Swing @ KS on the Keys features eight of the Ottawa area's best big bands: Aardvark Big Band, Big Band Ottawa, the Grey Jazz Big Band, the Main & Abbott Dance Band, Red Woodshed, Standing Room Only, The Stan Clark Orchestra and The Wild Cards, plus smaller swing bands including the Apex Jazz Band, the Kitchissippi Jazz Kats and the Ottawa Pocket Jazz Band.

Our dining room has high ceilings, plenty of room for the band and a large dance floor, reminiscent of the great ballrooms of the big band era.

Regular Sunday Swing Nights

$5 per person cover charge, added to your bill. Reservations strongly recommended: call 613-521-0498 or book online.

Getting Here

Getting here is easy with our own free parking lot, overflow parking at the cinema lot across the road, and the O-Train Line 2 stop at South Keys Shopping Centre.

KS on the Keys Ottawa · Est. 1971

Founded May 1971, KS on the Keys has brought Ottawa South together around great food ever since. All chicken and beef is 100% halal. Free on-site parking.
